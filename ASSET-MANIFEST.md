Photographic room scenes are real photographs served from local WebP files. Game art is sourced from the corresponding publisher pages. Third-party artwork is linked back to its source where it appears. The attached passport photo was not copied into the site or used to produce any site image.

## Original elements

The miniature house, waving character, driving mini-game, room transitions, contribution-calendar presentation, and all small games are drawn or rendered by the site code. Game status labels distinguish the profile-snapshot current game from titles on the wish list; no account data is synced.
